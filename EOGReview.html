RL.7.3: Character Interaction

🗝️ Locker #1: "Rocks in My Pocket"

Context: "...The official shook his head. 'I'm sorry, young lady, but you don't meet the weight requirement. No permit today.' Sophie's heart sank. She turned away from the desk without a word. Sophie walked dejectedly homeward, depositing rocks in each trash can that she passed. When she told the family what had happened, Papa was shocked." [cite: 1]

Question: Sophie feels defeated after the official denies her request for a work permit. Which sentence from the selection supports this statement? [cite: 1]

  • A. "Sophie was nervous when she and Judith entered the City Hall..."
  • B. "She was glad that he did not seem to take much interest in her."
  • C. "Sophie walked dejectedly homeward, depositing rocks in each trash can that she passed." (Correct)
  • D. "When she told the family what had happened, Papa was shocked."
🔓 Unlock the Rationale

The Trap: Option A describes an emotion, but it’s how she felt before the interaction. Option D shows a reaction, but it belongs to Papa, not Sophie. [cite: 1]

The Win: According to the i-Ready Teacher Guide, the word "dejectedly" is the smoking gun. It directly links her physical action (walking home) to her internal feeling of defeat after being denied. [cite: 1]
