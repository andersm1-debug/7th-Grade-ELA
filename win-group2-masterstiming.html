Group 2: The Master's Timing

Proficient Extension • Character Motive & Pacing Subtext

[¶1] The brass bell above the door chimed sharply, interrupting the rhythmic ticking that filled Julian’s repair shop. A man in a tailored suit stepped inside, checking his smartwatch twice before he even reached the counter. He placed a tarnished 1920s pocket watch onto the velvet mat. "I need this fully restored," the man said, tapping his finger impatiently against his phone screen. "I’m flying to Tokyo on Thursday. Can you expedite it?"

[¶2] Julian didn't reach for the watch immediately. He adjusted his jeweler's loupe and studied the frantic micro-movements of the executive standing before him. "I cannot expedite a century of rust, Mr. Vance," Julian said softly. "The mainspring must be soaked in oil for three days. If I force the gears to turn before they are ready, the teeth will shear completely."

[¶3] Vance frowned, clearly unaccustomed to hearing the word 'no'. "I’ll pay double your usual rate. Rush delivery."

[¶4] Julian gently picked up the pocket watch, feeling the heavy, cold brass in his palm. It had belonged to someone who understood that time was a companion to be walked alongside, not an obstacle to be sprinted past. "You misunderstand the transaction," Julian replied, handing the watch back across the counter. "You are paying for my patience, not my speed. If you cannot wait for the oil to seep, this is not the shop for you."
