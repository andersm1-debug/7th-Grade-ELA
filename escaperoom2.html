It requires almost zero prep for your teammate, but it forces students to debate and defend their answers under pressure. **The Setup (5 Minutes)** 1. Print a few copies of the 15-question "The Brook & A Poison Tree" deck. 2. Cut the questions into individual strips. Keep them in 15 separate piles at the teacher's desk. 3. Group students into teams of 3 or 4. Each team needs one dry-erase whiteboard and a marker. 4. The teacher sits at the front desk and acts as **"The Bouncer."** **The Rules of the Game** * **The Runner:** When the timer starts, each team sends one "Runner" up to the Bouncer to grab Question 1. The Runner takes it back to their group. * **The Debate:** The team reads the text, debates the answer, and writes the correct letter on their whiteboard. * **The Defense:** The Runner takes the whiteboard and the question strip back to the Bouncer. The Bouncer checks the answer. * *If it is WRONG:* The Bouncer simply says, "Try again," and sends the Runner back to their team. * *If it is RIGHT:* The Bouncer asks the Runner to explain *why* it is right or what the "trap" was. If the Runner can defend it, the Bouncer takes Question 1 and hands them Question 2. * **The Rotation:** Teams MUST rotate their Runner for every single question so one student doesn't do all the walking and talking. * **The Winner:** The first team to collect and solve all 15 questions wins the station. --- ### 🔐 The Grammar & Syntax Escape Room (HTML Code) This code takes all 8 locks from your original Google Form and turns them into a sleek, interactive Escape Room for your Canvas Hub. **Why this is better than a Google Form:** * **No "Spacebar" Traps:** I added JavaScript that automatically removes accidental spaces and converts everything to lowercase, so if a 7th grader types `Pizza` or `CAB`, they won't get locked out. * **Instant Feedback:** The inputs instantly turn green and lock in when correct, giving that satisfying "unlocked" feeling without needing to click through multiple form sections. **How to use it:** Create a new file in your GitHub repository named `EscapeRoom.html` and paste the following code exactly as it is.
