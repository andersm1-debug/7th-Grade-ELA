Group 3: The Red Beacon

Strategic Scaffold • Plot Summary & Motive

[¶1] Rain lashed against the windshield of the patrol truck. Ranger Maya gripped the steering wheel, peering through the foggy glass into the blackness of Blackwood Gorge. It was past midnight, and the radio sputtered with static. A lone teenage hiker was missing along the ridge trail, hours after an unexpected flash flood washed out the footbridge.[cite: 10]

[¶2] Maya knew standard rescue protocol: wait for the storm to clear before hiking into the ravine. But rising creek waters were already chewing away at the dirt embankments. If the hiker was trapped near the riverbank, waiting until sunrise would be too late. She grabbed her pack: thermal blankets, a loud whistle, and a flare.[cite: 10]

[¶3] Stepping out into the gale, Maya fought through mud along the canyon rim. Above the roar of rushing water, she blew three sharp, piercing blasts on her whistle—the universal signal for distress. She paused, straining to listen. From deep down in the dark gorge, a tiny flicker of red light blinked back three times.[cite: 10]
