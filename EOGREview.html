🎯 EOG Strategy: The Rationale Locker

On the Reading EOG, picking the right answer is about more than just reading—it's about attacking the distractors. Use these lockers to see how the test-makers think.

Target Standard: RL.7.3 (Plot & Character Interaction)

🗝️ Locker #1: "Rocks in My Pocket"

Context Excerpt: "...The official shook his head. 'I'm sorry, young lady, but you don't meet the weight requirement. No permit today.' Sophie's heart sank. She turned away from the desk without a word. Sophie walked dejectedly homeward, depositing rocks in each trash can that she passed. When she told the family what had happened, Papa was shocked."

Question: Sophie feels defeated after the official denies her request for a work permit. Which sentence from the selection supports this statement?

  • A. "Sophie was nervous when she and Judith entered the City Hall..."
  • B. "She was glad that he did not seem to take much interest in her."
  • C. "Sophie walked dejectedly homeward, depositing rocks in each trash can that she passed." (Correct)
  • D. "When she told the family what had happened, Papa was shocked."
🔓 Unlock the Test-Maker's Rationale

The "Trap": Students often pick Option A because "nervous" is an emotion, but the question asks for a feeling after the denial. Option D is a common trap because it shows a strong emotion, but it belongs to Papa, not Sophie.

The Win: According to the i-Ready Guide, the word "dejectedly" is the key piece of evidence that directly links the event (denial) to Sophie's defeated internal state.
