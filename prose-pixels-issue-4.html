Edition 4: AI + WIN = WIN

Hey Team!

As we push through the spring semester, finding extra minutes in the day feels nearly impossible. For Edition 4 of Prose & Pixels, we are tackling the daily WIN block — specifically, how AI can handle the heavy lifting of designing these activities so you can focus your energy where it matters most.

1. The Coffee Break Plan

WIN time is our chance to reach kids exactly where they are, but finding the time to draw up a differentiated playbook on top of your core classes can be incredibly time-consuming.

You already leave it all on the field for your primary lessons, and your planning time is finite. What if you could reclaim that time?

In the few minutes it takes to pack a reusable K-Cup and let your morning mug brew, you could have your entire WIN block planned, leveled, and ready to deploy.

2. The Paradigm Shift

What if we flipped the script?

Instead of WIN time being a drain on your planning period, imagine it as the space where you deploy a high-speed teaching assistant. AI is not just another tech initiative to learn; it is a co-teacher ready to take the heavy lifting of differentiation completely off your plate.

You step back into the role of facilitator and let AI build the custom materials your students need.

3 Ways AI Can Plan WIN Time For You

Instant Leveled Texts

Found a strong article but know half your WIN group will struggle with the vocabulary?

Paste it into your AI tool and ask it to rewrite the text at a 5th-grade and a 9th-grade reading level. Now the whole group can discuss the same topic while reading at their own comfort level.

Targeted Skill Practice

Need a quick intervention on a specific skill?

Ask AI to write a high-interest paragraph about a trending video game, current sports star, or student-friendly topic, followed by targeted questions on skills like main idea or citing evidence.

Automated Choice Boards

Type in your focus standard and ask AI to generate a three-option choice board.

For example: one creative task, one analytical task, and one hands-on task. Instant differentiated engagement with almost no formatting required.

Teacher Prompt of the Week

Copy and paste this prompt when you need a quick WIN activity focused on textual evidence.

Act as a middle school intervention specialist. Write a highly engaging, 150-word mystery story about a missing artifact. Then, generate 3 multiple-choice questions focusing on citing textual evidence. Provide an answer key with brief explanations for why the incorrect distractors are wrong.

Prompt Repository Quick Hits

Keep these quick-hit prompts in your back pocket for zero-prep WIN days.

The Simplifier

Take this text and rewrite it for a student reading at a 4th-grade level, keeping the core concepts intact.

The Hook

Write a 5-question trivia game about [Topic] to introduce our new unit.

The Debate Starter

Give me 3 controversial, middle-school-appropriate debate prompts related to [Standard/Topic] that require students to pick a side.

Student Benefits

When we let AI generate targeted tasks, students thrive. They get immediate, low-stakes practice tailored to their exact interests and skill needs.

Students can also use AI as a Socratic tutor by asking it for hints or step-by-step explanations rather than simply waiting for the right answer.

That turns intervention into active, self-paced discovery.

The AI Champion Weekly Challenge

Our challenge to you this week is gentle: try just one of these strategies.

The Challenge: Grab the Prompt of the Week, paste it into your AI tool, and use exactly what it gives you.

The Reward: Let AI do the work so you can buy yourself 15 minutes of peace during your prep period today.

You have more than earned it.
