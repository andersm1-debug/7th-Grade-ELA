Edition 4: AI + WIN = WIN

Hey Team!

As we push through the spring semester, finding extra minutes in the day feels nearly impossible. For Edition 4 of Prose & Pixels, we are tackling WIN (What I Need) time and exploring how AI can handle the heavy lifting of planning differentiated activities.

1. The Coffee Break Plan

WIN time gives us a chance to meet students exactly where they are, but planning differentiated activities on top of everything else can be exhausting.

Imagine using the few minutes it takes your morning coffee to brew to generate an entire WIN lesson that is already leveled, differentiated, and ready to use.

2. The Paradigm Shift

Instead of viewing WIN time as another planning burden, think of AI as a high-speed teaching assistant.

You become the facilitator while AI generates customized texts, activities, interventions, and extension tasks that match student needs.

3 Ways AI Can Plan WIN Time For You

Instant Leveled Texts

Paste a challenging article into AI and ask it to rewrite the text at multiple reading levels.

Students can discuss the same content while reading at a level that matches their needs.

Targeted Skill Practice

Ask AI to generate short passages and questions focused on a specific skill.

Example: Main idea practice using a passage about a popular video game, athlete, or current event.

Automated Choice Boards

Give AI a standard and ask it to generate a three-option choice board.

One creative task, one analytical task, and one hands-on task instantly.

Teacher Prompt of the Week

Act as a middle school intervention specialist. Write a highly engaging, 150-word mystery story about a missing artifact. Then generate 3 multiple-choice questions focusing on citing textual evidence. Provide an answer key with brief explanations for why the incorrect distractors are wrong.

Prompt Repository Quick Hits

  • The Simplifier: "Take this text and rewrite it for a student reading at a 4th-grade level while keeping the core concepts intact."
  • The Hook: "Write a 5-question trivia game about [Topic] to introduce our new unit."
  • The Debate Starter: "Give me 3 controversial, middle-school-appropriate debate prompts related to [Standard/Topic] that require students to pick a side."

Student Benefits

When AI generates targeted tasks, students receive immediate practice tailored to their interests and skill levels.

AI can also act as a Socratic tutor, offering hints, explanations, and guided questioning rather than simply providing answers.

This transforms intervention into active, self-paced learning.

The AI Champion Weekly Challenge

Try just one strategy from this newsletter.

Copy the Prompt of the Week, paste it into your favorite AI tool, and use whatever it generates with students.

Let the AI do the work and reclaim a few minutes of your planning time.

You've more than earned it.
