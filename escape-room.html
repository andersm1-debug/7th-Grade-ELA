🔒 EOG Breakout: The Locked Library

Lock 1: The Word Smith

You are locked in the archives. The only way out is through the main door, which is secured by three digital padlocks. To open the first lock, you must decipher the structural code of a word.
"I am a word made of two parts. My prefix means 'against' or 'opposite'. My root word means 'poison'. What word am I?"

Hint: Think of what a doctor gives you if you are bitten by a venomous snake.

Lock 2: The Multiple Choice Combo

The first padlock clicks open. The second padlock requires a 3-letter combination based on your knowledge of plot elements.
Read the clues and select the correct letter for each to form your 3-letter code.

1. The turning point or most exciting moment of a story is the:
A) Exposition    B) Climax    C) Resolution

2. The perspective from which a story is told is the:
X) Point of View    Y) Main Idea    Z) Theme

3. An internal conflict takes place:
D) Between two characters    E) Against nature    F) Inside a character's mind

Lock 3: The Figurative Formula

Only one lock remains. This padlock requires a 3-digit numeric code. You must analyze the paragraph below to find the numbers.
"The classroom was a freezer. The wind howled outside the window, begging to come in. The students sat as still as statues, waiting for the bell to ring."

Digit 1: How many Metaphors are in the text?
Digit 2: How many examples of Personification are in the text?
Digit 3: How many Similes are in the text?

🔓 ESCAPED!

Congratulations! You have successfully deciphered all the codes and escaped the library. You are officially ready for the EOG.
