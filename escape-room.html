The Dancing Field

The Mission Question: How does the author's use of the words "fluttering" and "dancing" impact the meaning of the stanza?

  • D: They explain the literal, scientific life cycle of a daffodil blowing in the wind.

Lock 4: RL.7.6 (Point of View / Narrator Reliability)

Hearken! and observe how healthily—how calmly I can tell you the whole story."

Choose the correct option. Then, pinpoint the specific **adjective** in the text used to describe the narrator's highly sharpened, sharp sense of hearing. Type that single word below.
