The Final Signal

Unit 2: Active Comprehension • Plot Synthesis & Inference

[¶1] Leo tapped the glass of the handheld barometer, watching the red needle shudder and drop another three millibars. Outside the small wooden boat repair shack, the sound of the Atlantic surf had changed from a rhythmic lap to a low, angry rumble. The harbor sirens had sounded forty minutes ago, urging every vessel back behind the sea wall before the gale hit.

[¶2] He checked his watch: 4:15 PM. His grandfather's battered skiff, The Tern, was still moored out on the exposed north slip instead of the sheltered inner dock. Grandfather had driven into town to fetch spare engine gaskets, assuming the squall was still half a day away.

[¶3] Leo knew the harbor rules: no minors on the docks when storm flags were flying. Yet, if the wind swung northeast as the barometer predicted, The Tern would splinter against the pilings before Grandfather made it back over the bridge. Pulling his hood tight against the stinging salt spray, Leo reached for the heavy dock line coiled on the peg, shouldered the shop door open, and stepped into the gale.
